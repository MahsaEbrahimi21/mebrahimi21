# Mahsa Ebrahimi

**Physics PhD Candidate at Harvard University**  
Experimental Materials Physics · Microfabrication · 3D Microscopy · Scientific Computing

[📄 View my CV](CV.md)

I am a Physics PhD candidate at Harvard University working at the intersection of experimental materials physics, microfabrication, microscopy, mechanics, and scientific computing. My research combines controlled experiments with particle-resolved imaging and computational modeling to study deformation and defects in crystalline materials.

## Technical interests

- Materials physics and defect mechanics
- Microfabrication and cleanroom processing
- 3D confocal microscopy and particle-resolved analysis
- Finite-element modeling and stress/strain analysis
- Scientific computing in Python and MATLAB
- Semiconductor process and materials R&D

## Technical toolkit

**Programming & analysis:** Python, MATLAB, NumPy  
**Simulation:** Abaqus, finite-element analysis  
**Microfabrication:** Photolithography, mask alignment, reactive ion etching (RIE)  
**Microscopy & analysis:** Spinning-disk confocal microscopy, OVITO, Huygens, Origin  
**Engineering:** Fusion 360, LaTeX

## Selected public projects
