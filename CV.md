## Research Experience

### Weitz Lab, Harvard University — Graduate Researcher | 2023–Present

- Design and fabricate photolithographic templates to control the structure and orientation of ~1.5 μm silica colloidal crystals.
- Develop controlled mechanical-deformation experiments to study defect dynamics and mechanical response in particle-resolved colloidal crystals.
- Acquire and analyze 3D spinning-disk confocal microscopy datasets to characterize particle configurations, crystal structure, defects, crystallographic orientation, and deformation.
- Develop Python and OVITO workflows for particle-level structural analysis, including defect identification, local elastic strain, crystal orientation, and crystallographic analysis.
- Build finite-element models in Abaqus to simulate stress and strain fields under controlled mechanical loading.
- Develop analytical and numerical models in MATLAB and Python to calculate local stress fields and interpret deformation experiments.
- Design custom experimental components and loading geometries using Fusion 360.

- Conducted experimental research on high-sensitivity magnetometry using nitrogen-vacancy centers in diamond and singlet infrared absorption.
- Built, aligned, and optimized optical measurement setups, including laser alignment, optical components, photodetectors, and signal-detection hardware.
